I · Strategic Product Foundation
Market Intelligence

The Problem & Differentiation

🕳️

The Market Gap

Independent, self-taught dancers are currently "digitally homeless". Mainstream social platforms prioritize fleeting virality over career-long networking, leaving a high noise-to-value ratio for serious artists.

🎯

The Opportunity

A dedicated platform that treats dance as a professional discipline, not just entertainment — providing structured identity, community, and career tools for serious artists.

💡

The Differentiation Logic

DanceMingle is positioned as a Legacy-Building Cultural Platform rather than a social app. By integrating a "Dance Archive" with professional networking, it transforms a creative hobby into a structured professional identity.

VI · Product Prioritization
Prioritization Framework

The MoSCoW Method

🔴 Must-Have

Dance Archive + Login / Register

These are the non-negotiable core of the MVP. Login/Register establishes the user database and trust infrastructure, while the Dance Archive delivers immediate, low-latency cultural value to the "Aspirant" persona — making first-visit retention defensible.

🟡 Should-Have

Live Payment Integration & Subscription Flow

While the MVP launched as Freemium, the "Should-Have" status ensured the Razorpay Live API and WooCommerce / PMS handshake were fully architected and tested for immediate monetization scalability — not deferred to a future sprint.

🟢 Could-Have

My Profile Page

Added for personalization and user identity. While secondary to core content, it serves a dual purpose: securing user data through Regex-based redirections and enabling the Auto Subscription Cancel flow — prioritized after the core archive content was stable.

🟣 Won't-Have (This Release)

Dance Prop Resale & Dance Connect

These features introduce significant logistical and social-density complexity. Strategically deferred to future releases to allow the primary "Culture" content layer to gain traction first — ensuring the community foundation is solid before marketplace dynamics are introduced.

VII · Metrics & Guardrails
Product Analytics

What We Measure

📈 Primary · Growth
DAU — Daily Active Users
Definition

The number of unique logged-in users accessing the platform daily — the core north star for measuring cultural stickiness and content effectiveness.

Tracking Method

Measured via the WordPress User Log or Ultimate Member login timestamps, cross-referenced with session data.

💰 Secondary · Monetization
Success Rate of Checkout
Definition

The percentage of users who initiate the checkout flow and successfully complete a payment — the direct signal of conversion funnel health.

Tracking Method

Calculated by comparing Razorpay "Captured" payments against WooCommerce "Pending" orders to identify drop-off at each stage.

🛡️ Guardrail · Quality
Webhook Delivery Failures
Definition

The number of failed automated order completions after a payment is successfully captured — the most critical quality signal post-launch.

Threshold
Must remain at ZERO

If a user pays but the Webhook fails to unlock the feature, the GTM strategy is immediately halted to protect brand trust.
